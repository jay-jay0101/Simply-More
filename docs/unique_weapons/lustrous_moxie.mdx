Attack Speed: 1.4

### Unique Effect: Radiant Mark
◇ Chance on hit to apply Radiant Mark. This effect stacks, but the duration is reduced for each stack.

◇ For each stack of Radiant Mark on an enemy, deal additional true damage.


### Right Click:
◇ After a short windup, teleport to the closest foe inflicted with Radiant Mark, dealing AOE damage.

◇ You are stunned after using this ability.
