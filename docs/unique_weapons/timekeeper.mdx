```
Weapon Type: 1H Khopesh
Repair Ingredient: Runic Tablet
Attack Damage: 8
Attack Speed: 2
```

### Unique Effect: Curse of Ra
◇ Only available during the day.
◇ Chance on hit to lighten the area around you making all entities within 30 blocks glow.


### Right Click:
◇ Blast a solar flare forwards, which blinds, burns and pushes away enemies.


-


### Unique Effect: Curse of Khonsu
◇ Only available during the night.
◇ Chance on hit to gain speed, become invisible and inflict darkness.


### Right Click:
◇ Fire a gravitational pull, launching foes upwards and slowing them greatly


-


### Unique Effect: Curse of Thoth
◇ Only available in a Timeless dimension, such as the Nether or End.
◇ Chance on hit to trigger either Curse of Ra or Curse of Khonsu
