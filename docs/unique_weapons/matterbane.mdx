```
Weapon Type: 1H Longsword
Repair Ingredient: Runic Tablet
Attack Damage: 9
Attack Speed: 1.6
```

### Unique Effect: Negative Energy
◇ Chance on hit to spawn a particle which travels through walls and shields, dealing damage.


### Right Click:
◇ Spawn a rift which connects to enemies within 10 blocks, preventing enemies from leaving the range.

◇ Rifts disappear after 15 seconds, or if you move more than 15 blocks away.
