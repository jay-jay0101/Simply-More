```
Weapon Type: 2H Claymore
Repair Ingredient: Runic Tablet
Attack Damage: 12
Attack Speed: 1.2
```

### Unique Effect: False Appearance
◇ While held, gain an aura which slows and weakens enemies the longer they stay inside it.

◇ Holding this weapon slows you down.


### Right Click:
◇ Solidify for 2.5 seconds, during which you cannot attack nor move, but gain extra defense.

◇ If you are hit during this, you return to normal and stun others inside your aura.
