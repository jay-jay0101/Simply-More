```
Weapon Type: 1H Dagger
Repair Ingredient: Runic Tablet
Attack Damage: 8
Attack Speed: 2.2
```

### Unique Effect: Ancient Occult
◇ Chance on hit to raise the duration of Hex.


### Right Click:
◇ Target someone, if they do not have Hex, hex them. The duration of hex is increased the more positive status effects they have.

◇ Hex debuffs the opponent, getting worse over time, the longer it is applied.

◇ If the target does have Hex, expel it and gain temporary health.
