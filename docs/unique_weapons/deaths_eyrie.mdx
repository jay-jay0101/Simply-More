```
Weapon Type: 2H Claymore
Repair Ingredient: Runic Tablet
Attack Damage: 12
Attack Speed: 0.8
```

### Unique Effect: Murder's Heed
◇ While held, summon a crow to follow you.

◇ Chance on hit to inflict bleed and summon another crow, up to 5. The length of the bleed increases the more crows you have


### Right Click:
◇ Command your crows to attack a single target, blinding and damaging them.

◇ Damage dealt by your crows is converted into healing for you.

◇ After attacking, you may not gain more crows until the cooldown ends.
