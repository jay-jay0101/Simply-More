```
Attack Speed: 1.9
```

### Unique Effect: Hunter's Instincts
◇ The more hungry you are, the faster you move and the more damage you deal.


### Right Click:
◇ Grasp an enemy, sapping hunger and health to fill your hunger bar.

◇ If you fill your hunger bar, you begin to fill your health.

◇ After 5 seconds, split the target away.

◇ While grasping, you may not attack, and your target gains weakness and mining fatigue.
