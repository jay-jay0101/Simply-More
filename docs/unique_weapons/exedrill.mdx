```
Weapon Type: 1H Lance
Repair Ingredient: Runic Tablet
Attack Damage: 9
Attack Speed: 1
```

### Unique Effect: Excavation
◇ Hitting an enemy will heat this weapon up.

◇ Chance on hit to tremble the ground, causing slowness and mining fatigue to those nearby. This will also heat the weapon up greatly.

◇ When the weapon becomes too heated, it will cause an earthquake which thorws enemies away.


### Right Click:
◇ Throw many rocks, which stun enemies hit.
