### Unique Effect: Shattering Edge
◇ This weapon breaks shields.
◇ Chance on hit to inflict stacking Armor Crunch, making an opponent's armor less effective.


### Right Click:
◇ After a short charge, slam downwards, throwing enemies nearby upwards, and inlicting weakness, slowness and Armor Crunch.
