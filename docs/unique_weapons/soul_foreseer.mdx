Attack Damage: 9
Attack Speed: 1.4

### Unique Effect: Soul Foresight
◇ Chance on hit to steal an enemy's soul.

◇ While the target has their soul stolen, positive effects drain much faster.


### Right Click:
◇ Return the soul to the target, blinding, slowing and teleporting them to you.
