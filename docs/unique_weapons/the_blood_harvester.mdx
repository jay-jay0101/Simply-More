Weapon Type: 1H Scythe
Repair Ingredient: Runic Tablet

### Unique Effect: Blood Sense
◇ This weapon has 10% lifesteal.


### Right Click:
◇ Enter a harvest for 15 seconds.

◇ While inside of a harvest, gain 20% lifesteal, speed, haste and begin to inflict bleed.

◇ While inside of a harvest, you can see enemies nearby.
