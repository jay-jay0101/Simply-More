```
Weapon Type: 1H Lance
Repair Ingredient: Runic Tablet
Attack Damage: 9
Attack Speed: 1
```

### Unique Effect: Supernova
◇ Chance on hit to gain stacks of Starlight, up to 10.

◇ While you have stacks of Starlight, you gain random bursts of speed.

◇ The chance to gain stacks is increased while mounted.


### Right Click:
◇ Charge to consume all of your Starlight to explode, damaging all players nearby.

◇ You take greatly reduced damage to this explosion.
