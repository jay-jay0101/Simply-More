```
Weapon Type: 1H Deer Horns
Repair Ingredient: Runic Tablet
Attack Damage: 8
Attack Speed: 3.8
```

### Unique Effect: Aging Mechanisms
◇ Hitting targets causes this weapon to oxidize, lowering its swing speed.

◇ Chance on hit to summon a jet of steam which flings people up.


### Right Click:
◇ Gradually remove the oxidization from the weapon.

◇ This may fire off stunning sparks at enemies.
