### Unique Effect: Wither and Bloom
◇ Each hit with this weapon grants Bloom, up to 15. The more bloom you have, your defenses will be gradually buffed.


### Right Click:
◇ Consume 8 stacks of Bloom to create a flower field, buffing allies inside.
