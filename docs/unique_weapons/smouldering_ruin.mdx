```
Weapon Type: 1H Backhand Blade
Repair Ingredient: Runic Tablet
Attack Damage: 7
Attack Speed: 2.3
```

### Unique Effect: Doomsayer
◇ Chance on hit to inflict wither.

◇ If the target has Withering Fate, increase its level.


### Right Click:
◇ Jump backwards and float in the air.

◇ If you face towards an entity, teleport to them and slash the air, dealing damage and inflicting Withering Fate.

◇ Withering Fate reduces max hp.
